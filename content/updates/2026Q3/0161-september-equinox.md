Today marks the September equinox (yesterday if you're west of UTC), the moment when the Sun crosses the celestial equator moving southward, resulting in nearly equal hours of day and night across the globe and marking the start of astronomical autumn in the Northern Hemisphere https://janmr.com/posts/equinoxes-and-solstices/
