The term "regression" in Regression Analysis comes from Sir Francis Galton, who in the 1870s-80s observed that offspring characteristics (originally in sweet peas, later confirmed in human height) tend to regress toward the population mean rather than resembling their parents' extremes https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regression_toward_the_mean
